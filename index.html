About the promotion

LoCo Pro Wrestling started in 2017 as a single show built specifically for Longmont. Thom Ingram, a Longmont high school teacher, and Aaron Macomber had met working at an Apple Store, found they were both wrestling fans, and trained together at Primos Premier Pro Wrestling's Butcher Shop in Denver as the tag team Cosmos and Chronos. What bothered them was that there was nothing closer to home. Ingram booked the Dickens Opera House, brought in students he knew as the crew, and built a Longmont-specific story with them: two factions fighting over the founding of the city itself.

Battle for Main Street was meant to be the only one. The promotion went dormant, Ingram moved away, and Macomber took it over and brought it back to the Dickens Opera House in November 2025, eight years after the first bell. The factions and the Longmont mythology carried over, and full matches now publish free on YouTube between shows.

The storyline

This section describes the fictional on-screen story, not the business. It is provided as background for anyone writing about the show.

The current arc follows two factions. The Rising won the 2025 City of Lights Tournament. At Vendetta, J.T. Staten won the City of Lights Championship and dissolved his own faction to form The Foundation, taking control of the venue and locking the rest of the roster out. The Last Stand at the Lodge is the answer to that.

Roster highlights

The full active roster. Match history is available on request. Every photo below except The Vigilante is print resolution when clicked, and all of those require the CMRice Photography credit.

Johnny Crash

Longmont's hometown wrestler and a punk-rock brawler. Reached the final two of the City of Lights Elimination Gauntlet at Vendetta.

J.T. Staten

Current City of Lights Champion and the promotion's lead antagonist. Leader of The Foundation.

Zeak Gallent

"The Picture of Talent." Won the City of Lights Tournament in 2025, pictured here with the trophy. Known for his promo work.

Erza Menagerie Tinker

Central to the storyline across both Opera House shows, including a match she was forced into against her own partner.

Carter Cash

"The Future." Turned on his own side in the closing moments of Vendetta, in complete silence, and has not explained why.

Nicky Hyde

"The Stupendous One." Enforcer, and one half of a long-running feud with Carter Cash.

Codah Alexander

"The Skywalker." High-risk, high-flying style, which is where the name comes from.

Major Glory

The promotion's patriotic character, and one of its most reliable crowd favorites.

Michael Avalon

"The Gift of Greatness." His Vendetta win forced J.T. Staten into the Gauntlet personally instead of sending a proxy.

Niles Blood

Debuted at Vendetta by running in to break up a two-on-one attack, and stayed.

Dean Mercer

"All Business." His Falls Count Anywhere win over Johnny Crash at Vendetta earned his side the right to pick the referee.

Franky Gonzales

"The Pretty Boy." Thrown out of the Gauntlet by his own faction after a loss, and still calls himself a Pillar.

Cody Devine

"Cloud King." The original faction leader, who fights with contracts and loopholes more than holds.

Anuka Gutierrez

Fought at Vendetta for his own contractual freedom, won it, and left the match a free agent.

Hussy Steele

An enforcer, and the opponent standing between Anuka Gutierrez and that freedom.

Morgana Lavey

Recently escaped from the Netherrealm to compete at Vendetta.

Adam Starling

Commissioner of LoCo Pro Wrestling. The on-screen authority figure and the promotion's in-story spokesperson.

The Vigilante

A masked figure from the promotion's 2017 season whose influence is still felt today. His stated goal was to bring down The Pillars. Somebody else got there first.
